How it works

Three simple steps

Designed for students, interns, and short-term visitors comparing stays in Lusaka.

Search by destination

Pick Evelyn Hone, NIPA, ZCAS, or another point. Filter by stay type, dates, and guest count.

Compare location & commute

See property details and map-based distance. OpenStreetMap and routing come in Milestone 2 and 5.

Request a booking

Accounts and booking requests are planned for Milestone 4–5. No payments in the first version.

Trust & pricing

Verification and clear prices

Administrators will approve listings before a property shows as verified. This milestone shows layout only.

✓

K

Prices in Zambian kwacha

Location & commute

Know the journey, not just the address

We will show straight-line distance separately from road-route distance and estimated travel time when routing is connected. No live traffic or minibus timetables unless a real data source is added.
