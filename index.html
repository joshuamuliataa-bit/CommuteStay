How it works

Three simple steps

Designed for students, interns, and short-term visitors comparing stays in Lusaka.

Search by destination

Pick Evelyn Hone, NIPA, ZCAS, or another point. Filter by stay type, dates, and guest count.

Compare location & commute

Each stay shows photos, facilities, its place on the map and the estimated driving distance and time to your destination.

Request a stay

Log in and send a request that carries the commute you were shown, then arrange a viewing with the owner. CommuteStay takes no payments, so never pay before you have seen the room.

Trust & pricing

Verification and clear prices

An administrator reviews every listing before it goes live, and prices are always shown in kwacha.

✓

Administrator verification

The Admin-reviewed badge means an administrator checked the listing’s details before it was published. It does not mean the property has been physically inspected.

K

Prices in Zambian kwacha

Prices are set by the property owner and shown as K (ZMW). Confirm the final price and what it includes with the owner before you pay anything.

Location & commute

Know the journey, not just the address

Each stay shows the road distance and estimated driving time to Evelyn Hone, NIPA and ZCAS, worked out from OpenStreetMap road data. These are route-based estimates. They do not include live traffic or minibus waiting times.
